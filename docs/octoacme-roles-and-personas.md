# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Delivery Lead

### Role Summary
The Delivery Lead bridges strategy and execution by coordinating day-to-day delivery activities across the team. They work closely with the Project Manager, developers, QA, and stakeholders to keep work flowing smoothly toward each milestone.

### Responsibilities
- Track sprint progress and flag blockers early
- Coordinate handoffs between developers, QA, and release management
- Own the release readiness checklist and go/no-go decisions
- Escalate risks and dependencies to the Project Manager
- Support retrospectives and drive process improvements

### Goals
- Ensure each iteration ships on time and meets quality gates
- Reduce cross-functional handoff friction
- Maintain a clear, shared picture of delivery status

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and blocker triage with the team
- Delivery status reports to the Project Manager
- Coordination threads with QA and release engineers before each release

### Key Interactions
- **Project Manager** – receives scope and schedule guidance; escalates risks upward
- **Developers** – unblocks day-to-day issues and tracks work completion
- **QA/Testing Lead** – aligns on test readiness and acceptance criteria coverage
- **Stakeholder Liaison** – provides delivery status for external communication
- **Product Managers** – confirms scope is understood and prioritised correctly

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
The QA/Testing Lead owns the test strategy for a project. They define acceptance criteria coverage, coordinate testing activities, and act as the quality gate before releases reach production.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain the overall test strategy and coverage goals
- Coordinate manual and automated testing activities across the team
- Validate that acceptance criteria are met before sign-off
- Track defects and drive resolution with developers
- Confirm release readiness and participate in go/no-go decisions

### Goals
- Prevent defects from reaching production
- Ensure every release meets agreed quality standards
- Build and improve shared automated test coverage over time

### Typical Communication
- Test plan documents shared with developers and Product Managers
- Defect triage sessions with developers
- Release readiness reports to the Delivery Lead and Project Manager

### Key Interactions
- **Delivery Lead** – provides release readiness status and participates in go/no-go
- **Developers** – coordinates defect resolution and test automation collaboration
- **Project Manager** – surfaces quality risks that could affect schedule or scope
- **Product Managers** – validates that acceptance criteria are testable and complete
- **Stakeholder Liaison** – communicates quality outcomes when required by stakeholders

---

## Stakeholder Liaison

### Role Summary
The Stakeholder Liaison is the primary point of contact between the project team and external stakeholders, dependent teams, or business partners. They consolidate and channel feedback to keep communication aligned and prevent information overload on both sides.

### Responsibilities
- Gather, consolidate, and prioritise stakeholder feedback
- Communicate project status, decisions, and risks to stakeholders
- Coordinate stakeholder review sessions and approval processes
- Ensure stakeholder concerns are represented in planning discussions
- Manage expectations and facilitate conflict resolution when needed

### Goals
- Keep stakeholders informed without disrupting team delivery focus
- Ensure stakeholder input reaches the team at the right time and in the right form
- Build trust between the team and its external partners

### Typical Communication
- Regular stakeholder briefings and written status summaries
- Feedback consolidation reports shared with Product Managers and PM
- Meeting facilitation for review or approval checkpoints

### Key Interactions
- **Project Manager** – aligns on communication plan and escalation paths
- **Product Managers** – channels stakeholder feedback into backlog and roadmap discussions
- **Delivery Lead** – receives delivery status to relay externally
- **QA/Testing Lead** – shares quality outcomes when stakeholders require assurance
- **Developers** – coordinates technical demonstrations or clarifications for stakeholders

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
The Technical Lead or Architect provides technical direction for the project. They guide implementation decisions, review architectural trade-offs, and ensure the solution is scalable, maintainable, and aligned with OctoAcme engineering standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define and communicate the technical approach and architecture
- Review designs, key pull requests, and technical trade-off proposals
- Identify technical risks and dependencies early in planning
- Guide developers on implementation patterns and best practices
- Collaborate with Product Managers and PM on feasibility and estimates

### Goals
- Deliver a technically sound, maintainable solution
- Reduce rework caused by architectural drift or unclear design decisions
- Ensure engineering decisions align with long-term product strategy

### Typical Communication
- Architecture decision records (ADRs) and technical design docs
- Code and design review comments
- Engineering updates in planning and retrospective meetings

### Key Interactions
- **Developers** – provides technical guidance, reviews code, and resolves design questions
- **Project Manager** – surfaces technical risks, dependencies, and schedule impacts
- **Product Managers** – advises on technical feasibility and trade-offs during roadmap planning
- **QA/Testing Lead** – aligns on testability requirements and test architecture
- **Delivery Lead** – flags technical blockers that affect delivery timelines
